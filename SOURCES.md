# Content provenance

The current release is a gameplay and visual preview. It has not completed the scientific presentation content or assignment rubric.

## Insect feeding

The Mountain Chicken Recovery Programme describes a mountain chicken pouncing on a large cricket and its field team feeding crickets to the frogs:

- [Trick or Treat! Spooky Snacks for our Mountain Chickens](https://www.mountainchicken.org/blog/trick-or-treat-spooky-snacks-for-our-mountain-chickens/), 3 November 2020. Checked 1 October 2026.

This supports using insect prey in the prototype. It does not establish that insects are the species' entire diet. Sprite shapes, prey placement, catch radius, speed, hop cooldown, and energy values are game design choices. The forest is an illustrative environment rather than a surveyed habitat map.

## Species and conservation

- [ZSL: Mountain chicken frog conservation](https://www.zsl.org/what-we-do/projects/mountain-chicken-frog-conservation), checked 1 October 2026. Supports the scientific name, Critically Endangered status, brown-and-cream camouflage, remaining wild population on Dominica, varied animal diet, wild monitoring, and protected breeding facilities.

The researcher and field station are illustrative game characters and scenery, not a depiction of a particular conservation facility. No population counts are presented.

## Art and fonts

All terrain and sprite textures are original code-drawn pixel art. The user-supplied Champion Island screenshot informed the pixel aesthetic; its assets are not included.

- [Press Start 2P](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/pressstart2p), by CodeMan38.
- [Pixelify Sans](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/pixelifysans), by the Pixelify Sans Project Authors.

Both fonts are bundled locally under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. License files accompany them in `public/assets/fonts/`.
